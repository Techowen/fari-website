Our Projects

Practical, community-owned programmes across education, women's enterprise and climate resilience — each one costed, tracked and reported on openly.

Flagship Programme

Back-to-School Scholarship Fund

Education

Keeping 240 girls in the classroom

Tuition, uniforms, textbooks and exam fees for a full academic year, paired with termly check-ins, a mentor for every student, and a hardship fund for families facing a sudden shock. Our retention rate across the programme currently sits at 86%.

  • Three local government areas in the FCT
  • ₦15,000 covers one student for one term
  • Mentorship continues through to graduation
