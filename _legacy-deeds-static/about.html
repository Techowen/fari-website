About Deeds Support

A community-led organisation working across the Federal Capital Territory and beyond — putting dignity, education and opportunity within reach of the people most often left out.

Our Story

It started with twelve girls and one classroom

In 2020 a small group of volunteers in Abuja pooled their own money to keep twelve girls from dropping out of secondary school. That first term worked — every one of them stayed enrolled. Word travelled, families came forward, and what began as an informal effort became Deeds Support Initiative International.

Five years on we run scholarship, enterprise and climate programmes across five communities. The method has not changed: sit with the people affected, design the programme together, publish what it cost and what it achieved, then come back next term.

Our Compass

Mission, vision & values

Three statements that decide what we take on — and what we turn down.

01

Our Mission

To empower, educate and support disadvantaged individuals — especially girls and women — with the tools, confidence and opportunity to shape their own futures, through programmes designed with the communities they serve.

02

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no child is denied an education because of poverty or gender, where women hold real economic power, and where every community is equipped to adapt and thrive in a changing climate.

Dignity

Nobody is a case number. We meet people as partners, never as recipients.

Accountability

Every naira is tracked and published. Our books are open to the communities we serve.

Partnership

Local leaders co-design and co-own each project from the first meeting.

Sustainability

Skills, assets and leadership stay in the community long after a project closes.

How We Work

Four steps, every single project

01

Listen

We start with community meetings — not proposals. Needs are named by the people who live them.

02

Co-design

Local leaders, teachers and beneficiaries shape the programme, the budget and the timeline.

03

Deliver

We implement with local partners, hiring and buying locally wherever it is possible.

04

Report

Spend and outcomes are published, reviewed with the community, and fed into the next cycle.
