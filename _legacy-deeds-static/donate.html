Questions

Before you give

Is my donation tax deductible?

We are a registered Nigerian NGO and issue an official receipt for every gift. Speak to your tax adviser about how it applies in your jurisdiction.

Can I choose which project to fund?

Yes — add the project name in the dedication field, or email us and we'll ring-fence your gift and report on it specifically.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, with one email and no questions asked. You keep full control of your giving.

Will I hear what happened?

Every donor gets our monthly field update and the full annual report — real numbers, including what did not work.
